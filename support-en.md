## FAQ

### Account

The App has no login or account system.

### Moving data to another device

Practice history, reed data, settings, and favorite target tones are stored on device.  
Deleting the App removes that data. There is no automatic transfer when you change phones.

### Subscriptions

- Buy from **Settings → Upgrade to Pro**, or from the prompt when you tap a Pro feature
- Restore from **Settings → Subscription**
- Cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

### Microphone & camera

- Microphone: used for pitch detection
- Camera: used only for the Pro mouth/reed panel

### Reed tracking

- Manage reeds in **Settings → Reed management**
- Optionally attach the reed you used on the practice preview or result screen
- Mark worn reeds as retired instead of deleting them so past history still resolves
